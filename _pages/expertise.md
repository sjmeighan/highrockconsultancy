# Expertise & Research

High Rock Consultancy focuses on the intersection of law, technology, financial crime and institutional governance. This page describes areas of research and expertise; it is not an offer to undertake regulated legal services or engagements incompatible with public duties.

## Anti-money laundering and financial intelligence

Money laundering law and policy, suspicious transaction reporting, intelligence systems, criminal investigation, asset recovery and the effectiveness of institutional responses.

## Crypto-assets and financial regulation

The regulation of crypto-assets, financial integrity risks, privacy-enhancing technologies, European regulatory developments, and socio-technical perspectives on financial intelligence.

## Regulation and governance

Regulatory design, policy implementation, organisational decision-making, systems thinking, and the tensions inherent in governing emerging technologies.

## Research and executive education

Research collaboration, academic writing, postgraduate teaching and specialist conference contributions concerning law, financial crime and technological transformation.
