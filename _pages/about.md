## Dr Steven Meighan, PhD, BL, MSc

Dr Steven Meighan is a barrister-at-law, researcher, author and senior law enforcement leader with extensive national and international experience in financial crime and organised crime investigations, financial intelligence and regulatory evaluation.

His international work includes participation in a Financial Action Task Force evaluation of Indonesia, as well as professional engagement with Europol, Interpol and European institutions. He holds a PhD addressing anti-money laundering and crypto-assets from a socio-technical systems perspective.

His work combines practical experience with legal and academic approaches to regulation and technological change.

## Featured books

**Money Laundering in Ireland: Context, History, and the Law** (Clarus Press, 2025) explores the development, context and legal framework of Irish money laundering regulation.

**Money Laundering and Crypto-Assets: A Socio-Technical Approach to Financial Intelligence** (Springer, 2026) examines crypto-laundering and regulatory responses through a socio-technical perspective.
